Genomics and Gene Regulation

The Genomics and Gene Regulation group at Cardiff University is interested in understanding how gene expression is regulated during both development and in a disease context, with a focus on cancer.

We use a number of computational and statistical approaches to ask and answer questions about biological systems, often in close collaboration with experimental biologists. Our research interests encompass a variety of themes, including understanding how the 3D structure around important transcription factors influences genome evolution and identifying the genomic and epigenetic features that determine whether a gene is regulated by a specific developmental signalling pathway.

Software & Code

We are committed to open, reproducible computational biology. All software tools and analysis pipelines developed in the lab are available on the Harmston Lab GitHub.

Packages & Tools

  • mLS-GKM —Efficient Multi-class Regulatory Sequence Classification with Gapped k-mer SVMs (arXiv 2026)
  • nf-CNE — A Nextflow pipeline for identifying conserved non-coding elements
  • DNAShaPy — A Python package for predicting DNA shape features from nucleotide sequences
  • ggbulksurv — An R package for streamlined survival analysis, statistical testing, and publication-ready plotting of Drosophila and C. elegans lifespan experiments (microPublication Biology, 2024).

Reproducible Research Repositories

Code, workflows, and custom scripts accompanying our individual studies—including regulatory domain annotation, Wnt-regulated gene expression analyses, and single-cell transcriptomics—are linked directly alongside each paper in the Publications section above and archived on GitHub.

Join the Lab

We are always keen to hear from motivated computational and quantitative researchers interested in gene regulation, genome evolution, and cancer genomics. We welcome applicants from a range of backgrounds—including biology, bioinformatics, computer science, statistics, and physics—who want to work closely alongside experimental collaborators.

  • PhD Students: Funded PhD projects in the group are regularly advertised through the Cardiff University School of Biosciences and the BBSRC South West Biosciences Doctoral Training Partnership (SWBio DTP) (typically opening in the autumn for entry the following October). We also welcome inquiries from prospective students applying for external studentships or fellowships.
  • Postdoctoral Researchers: While we do not currently have an open funded postdoc position, we are happy to support strong candidates interested in applying for independent fellowships (such as UKRI, Wellcome, EMBO, or Marie Skłodowska-Curie).
  • Undergraduate & MSc Students: If you are a current Cardiff University student looking for a final-year dissertation, MSc project, or summer computational placement, please get in touch early in the academic year to discuss available projects.
